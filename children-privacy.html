Children’s Privacy & Parent Notice

Ages 13+, local-only teen use, and regional safeguards.

Version 2026-10-23 · Updated 23 October 2026

On this page

  1. 1. Age band and local tools
  2. 2. India
  3. 3. EU/EEA, UK, United States and elsewhere
  4. 4. Parent and guardian requests
  5. 5. Safety and future changes

Launch audience: ages 13 and over. Ages 13–17 can use local focus tools only. Online features are restricted to self-declared adults aged 18+ in this release. We do not offer accounts for under-13s or claim to have verified a parent’s consent.

1. Age band and local tools

The app asks for an age band: under 13, 13–17, or 18 and over. It keeps that choice on the phone rather than collecting an exact birth date or identity document. Under-13 users are not allowed to continue through onboarding. A teen can continue without an account; Google sign-in, app cloud history backup and social use are unavailable. There is no advertising or automatic analytics/crash upload in this release.

Teen tools process sessions, permissions, counters and user-entered contacts on the device. Camera exercises are unavailable to under-18s because their pose SDK can send technical diagnostics. Its native auto-start is removed; initialization requires an adult's separate exercise notice acceptance. Math and reading challenges remain available. Parents and teens should understand the Accessibility disclosure, Android permissions and independent device backup settings. An age-band choice is not a guarantee of age or a replacement for required age assurance.

2. India

The DPDP framework treats people under 18 as children, subject to its applicable provisions, exceptions and phased commencement. A general “13+” statement does not replace the required child safeguards. Until a verified parental-consent process and any other required safeguards are implemented and reviewed, this app does not offer online child accounts. We do not ask a teen to bypass the restriction by checking a guardian-permission box.

3. EU/EEA, UK, United States and elsewhere

EU online-consent ages differ by country, and UK rules and age-appropriate design obligations can also apply. In the US, COPPA can apply to under-13 information, including where an operator has actual knowledge; state laws may protect older teens too. The current 18+ online restriction is a common product safeguard, not a statement that all countries have the same legal age or that an age screen alone establishes compliance.

4. Parent and guardian requests

If you believe a child supplied personal information to our online services, contact monkeymariners@gmail.com, addressed to Praduman Kumar, with enough information to locate it. Please do not email a child’s ID document, password or photo unless a proportionate verification method has first been agreed. We will investigate, restrict inappropriate processing and delete data where required, after checking the requester’s authority.

For local-only data, a parent or device owner can review permissions, clear app storage and manage independent Android/Google backups. We cannot erase a phone’s local files from an email request. See the account/data request route.

5. Safety and future changes

REATTAIN is not a child-supervision or guaranteed content-filtering product. Exercise challenges need appropriate supervision and suitability; they are optional and not medical advice. Any future online service for children must have the required notices, verifiable parental consent, limited collection, age-appropriate providers and other regional safeguards before it is enabled.
