This policy covers REATTAIN data held on your device, in app-requested Firebase services and in support records. It also explains the different treatment of Android system backups.

1. Retention schedule and criteria

CategoryHow long and why
Local history, settings, emergency contacts and local photosUntil you remove relevant content, clear app storage or uninstall, subject to separate OS backups. No automatic age-based purge is currently configured for general local history.
Transient screen observations, exercise frames and video fingerprintsUsed during the relevant operation in memory; not retained as a server history or recorded exercise video.
Firebase identity and optional cloud historyWhile the account/backup is maintained, until a verified deletion request is fulfilled or a justified legal exception applies. There is no configured general inactive-account expiry to rely on.
Published profiles, friendships, rooms and membersUntil deleted through the relevant action or a full account-data request. UI freshness/room expiry does not currently guarantee server deletion of all related records.
Support, privacy and abuse correspondenceAs needed to resolve the matter, demonstrate its handling, address a related dispute and satisfy specific legal duties. Any exceptional retention is limited to the relevant purpose; ask us for the basis and expected period for your request.
Purchase/financial records, if future sales are enabledAs required by applicable accounting, tax, fraud and dispute obligations. A provider can have separate statutory record duties. A fixed worldwide tax-retention period is not asserted here.
Host/provider security logs and residual backupsSubject to the service’s operational retention and security/legal requirements. We identify any relevant residual retention when handling a request, rather than promising immediate erasure from every backup.

2. Request account and associated-data deletion

Open Request Account & Data Deletion or email monkeymariners@gmail.com. You do not need to reinstall the app or pay to submit a request. Include the Google email or account identifier you used, if known, and say whether you want the whole account or selected data removed. We verify proportionately before disclosing or deleting another person’s data.

Our operational target is acknowledgement within 48 hours and completion or a substantive response within 30 days, subject to a shorter applicable deadline or a properly explained lawful extension. GDPR/UK GDPR calendar-month deadlines take priority. A request is handled manually; clicking an email link does not itself submit or complete deletion.

3. Scope and exceptions

Whole-account deletion covers the Firebase authentication identity and associated server data under our control: cloud sessions, challenges, daily totals, user-state records, published profile, friendship links, hosted rooms and membership records. Providers must also be instructed where required. We explain any narrowly retained legal/security record and its reason. We do not keep the deleted account usable merely by renaming it.

Our support team cannot remotely erase local-only data on a phone, an operating-system backup, Google’s independent account/payment records, or screenshots another person controls. You receive instructions for the actions you can take. Deleting only a social profile is not deleting the account.

4. Android system backups and re-upload prevention

Before clearing local storage, disable REATTAIN cloud backup and sign out on devices that could upload local history again. Complete the account-data request while you can still identify the account, especially if it is anonymous. After server deletion, clear app storage on each device with a local copy or uninstall it.

Android’s backup/restore system is independent of the app’s Firebase backup setting and may include eligible app data. Review your Google/device backup settings and remove relevant backups using the controls your provider offers. Restoring an old device backup can restore old local history and preferences. If a new adult account is then created and backup enabled, data may be uploaded again. We cannot promise that uninstalling erases every prior backup.

5. Subscriptions and complaints

No paid sales are enabled in this release. If you have a purchase from another version, cancel recurring billing with the store as well; account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate. A subscription does not prevent requesting deletion. See Refund & Cancellation and the grievance route.
