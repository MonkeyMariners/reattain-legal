Community Guidelines

Rules for profiles, room names and interactions when social tools are available.

Social tools are disabled in this release. These rules explain how existing social records and any future supported social use are handled. They apply to REATTAIN profiles, avatars, friend interactions and focus-room names/progress where those features are available. Any supported online social use is restricted to self-declared adults aged 18+ until appropriate child safeguards exist. It is not a private-messaging or child-supervision service. Read these rules with the acceptable-use terms.

1. Respect people and keep information safe

Do not threaten, bully, harass, target someone with hate or discrimination, impersonate another person, disclose private information, or pressure anyone to exercise or focus unsafely. Do not publish another person’s email, phone number, location, account credentials or private images.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation material, grooming or sexualization of minors; sexual violence or non-consensual intimate content.
  • Illegal content, credible threats, encouragement of violence or criminal activity, and instructions or encouragement to harm oneself or others.
  • Fraud, scams, spam, malware, credential theft, attempts to exploit the service, or evasion of a lawful moderation restriction.
  • Content that infringes copyright, trademark, privacy or another person’s rights; misleading claims of affiliation or endorsement.

3. What other users can see

A published profile contains a display name, preset avatar identifier, today’s total reel count and streak, plus account/update identifiers. Current cloud rules allow authenticated users to read published profiles and room/member records. A friend-only or room-members-only confidentiality promise does not apply. Use a nickname if you do not want your real name published. Removing a profile does not automatically erase every friendship, room record or copy made by someone else.

4. Report a concern

Report content or a user to monkeymariners@gmail.com with the profile/room identifier, relevant date and a short explanation. Avoid sending harmful material itself or unnecessary sensitive information. For an immediate threat, use appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement channels; this mailbox is not an emergency response service.

You can remove your profile or leave a room, but these actions are not represented as a full account deletion or a guaranteed block of another user. Full reporting/blocking and moderation controls must be available before a public social launch; this document alone is not such a control.

5. Review and enforcement

We assess reports proportionately, may request details, and can restrict/remove content or access where necessary and legally authorized. Actions can account for severity, safety, context, repeated conduct and applicable law. We aim to explain decisions where lawful and practicable. Request a review by replying to the decision or contacting Praduman Kumar through our grievance route. We do not promise continuous monitoring or an automatic room-record deletion system.
