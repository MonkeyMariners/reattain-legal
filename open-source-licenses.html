REATTAIN incorporates third-party software. Its components remain subject to their own copyright notices and licence conditions. The REATTAIN EULA does not replace or restrict rights granted by those licences.

1. Read software notices

In the app, open Settings → Legal → Open-source licences to read the licence registry bundled with that installed build. A downloadable Flutter/package notice snapshot is provided with these pages. Release builds can include different package/native components, so the installed notices and the relevant component licence apply.

2. Main component families

The project uses Flutter/Dart, Riverpod, GoRouter, Drift/SQLite, Firebase/Google Sign-In, camera and Google ML Kit pose detection, Android platform libraries, notifications, charting, billing and other utility packages. These examples are not an assertion that every component uses the same licence or that proprietary provider terms are open-source licences. Firebase, Google Play and other hosted services also have their own service/privacy terms.
