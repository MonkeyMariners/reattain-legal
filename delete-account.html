Request Account & Data Deletion

An external request route, including after you uninstall the app.

Request deletion of your REATTAIN account and associated server data. This service is operated by Praduman Kumar / MonkeyMariners. You can send the request without installing or signing in to REATTAIN.

Compose deletion request

If no email app opens, send an email yourself to monkeymariners@gmail.com with subject REATTAIN account and data deletion. The button only opens a draft; you must send the message. This page is not a form that silently submits a request.

In-app deletion controls

Updated Android builds provide Settings → Legal → Delete Data & Account. Delete all data clears this phone and, when a cloud identity is present, submits a request to remove its active cloud app records. A linked Google login is kept for data-only requests; an anonymous backup identity is included in its cleanup. Delete account permanently submits an authenticated request to remove the REATTAIN login and associated active cloud app records. It does not delete your Google/Gmail account.

Cloud cleanup is handled manually, with an operational target of 96 hours. A submission receipt confirms that we received the request; it does not mean cloud deletion is complete. Backup writes are paused while a request is pending. After the receipt, you choose whether to clear this phone immediately or keep its local data for now. Clearing storage closes the app and removes this device's app database, settings, credential caches and downloaded files. Other devices and independent OS/provider backups require separate action.

Cloud requests need internet; Google accounts require sign-in confirmation. If submission fails, the app does not report success or clear this phone automatically. Use the email route above if you cannot sign in, have an older build, or need help. You may submit a deletion request without paying or enabling cloud backup.

1. What to include

  1. Say that you want your REATTAIN account and associated data deleted, or identify the specific data you want removed.
  2. Provide the account email or user ID, if you know it. For an anonymous backup, contact us before clearing storage so we can help identify it.
  3. Send from the account email when possible. If you cannot access it, explain that; we will use proportionate verification. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, tokens, payment-card numbers or unnecessary identity documents.

2. What the whole-account request covers

Our deletion workflow covers the Firebase authentication identity and related cloud sessions, challenge results, daily progress, user state, published profile, friendship links, hosted rooms and membership records under our control. It includes a provider follow-up where legally required. We may need clarification to distinguish your records from another user’s records.

3. Timing and records that can remain

We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and complete deletion or give a substantive response within 30 days, subject to applicable law. If a calendar-month deadline, shorter deadline or permitted extension applies, that takes priority and we explain it. A restricted copy of records may be retained where a specific law, security need or existing legal claim requires it; we identify the categories, reason and expected period. Residual provider backups follow their applicable deletion cycles. See the full retention policy.

4. Local data and preventing restoration

Turn off app cloud backup and sign out on your devices, then clear app storage or uninstall after the request can be identified. Local-only data cannot be remotely erased by support. Review and remove Android/Google/device backups separately, because uninstalling does not necessarily remove them. Do not restore an old copy and re-enable backup if you want that data to remain deleted.

5. Purchases and help

No paid plans are sold by the current release. If an earlier or future version has a subscription, cancel it through Google Play separately to stop renewal. Deletion does not itself request a refund. You can still request deletion while a subscription exists. For problems submitting a request, use our support and grievance contact.
