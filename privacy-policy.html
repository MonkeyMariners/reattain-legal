4. Accessibility Service and sensitive permissions

The optional Android Accessibility Service is used for focus tools, not to assist users with disabilities. Before opening the permission settings, the app explains the access and asks you to continue or decline. You can revoke access in Android Settings at any time.

  • It observes the foreground app’s package name and feed-scroll events to block selected apps and count short videos.
  • On supported short-video screens, it can briefly read visible creator/caption text to form an in-memory fingerprint that distinguishes one video from another. Screen text and fingerprints are not uploaded as history.
  • For website blocking, it reads a supported browser’s address field and extracts the host for comparison with blocking rules. Other URL components can be present transiently while that host is extracted; they are not kept as browsing history.
  • For YouTube Study Mode, it compares the visible channel name with your chosen allowlist. The allowlist is saved locally.

The service is not designed to collect passwords, private messages, notification contents, files or arbitrary screen content. Diagnostic device logs can contain app package identifiers, timing and error information. Do not interpret local processing as a promise that the operating system creates no logs.

Usage access supplies foreground-app and screen-time information. Overlay access draws block screens. Do Not Disturb access manages session interruptions. Camera access is used for on-device exercise pose processing and the flashlight; exercise frames are not recorded or uploaded. The app does not request microphone or location access. Call permission supports calls to saved contacts; public emergency numbers open the dialer. Notifications, a foreground service, boot recovery and optional battery-exemption access support the running timer and reminders. Denying an optional permission limits its feature, and does not require accepting analytics or cloud backup.

5. Optional cloud backup and Android backup

App cloud backup is off by default, including for existing installs with no recorded choice. An eligible adult can enable it in Settings. Google sign-in and backup are separate choices. If enabled without Google sign-in, Firebase Anonymous Authentication can create a random account ID. Anonymous credentials can be lost when storage is cleared; contact us before doing that if you need help identifying cloud data.

Firebase history backup includes session identifiers, purpose, start/end times, planned and focused duration, completion status, discipline and challenge settings, the number of blocked apps, block/emergency/tamper event counts, challenge scores and outcomes, exercise type and repetition totals, daily activity totals, XP and streak information. It does not include goal text, app blocklists, blocked website lists, channel allowlists, per-app reel counts, reading passages, question answers, exercise frames, local profile photos or emergency contact details.

Turning backup off stops new app backup/restore requests; an operation already transmitted can finish. It does not erase an existing cloud copy. Use Request Account & Data Deletion for that. Ordinary sign-out is not deletion.

Android system backup is separate. The current Android backup rules allow eligible app databases, preferences and files to participate in Google/device backup and device-to-device transfer. That can include information excluded from our limited Firebase history backup. Your Android settings, device manufacturer and backup provider control that service. Uninstalling alone does not necessarily erase previously backed-up copies or stop restoration. See retention and device backups.

6. Friends and focus rooms

Friends and focus rooms are disabled in this release. The following explains records that can exist from another version, and the intended processing if social tools are offered after the necessary safeguards are in place. Publishing a social profile sends a user ID, display name, preset avatar ID, today’s total reel count, streak and update time to Firestore. Friend links contain participating user IDs. Room records include the room name, host, members and focus progress.

Published profiles and room/member records are readable by authenticated users under the current service rules; they are not guaranteed to be visible only to friends or current room members. Do not publish confidential information. The app does not offer private messaging. Removing a profile does not automatically remove friendships, hosted rooms, room membership records or screenshots another user has made. Request a full cleanup through our deletion contact. A room disappearing from the UI is not proof that its server record was erased. See Community Guidelines.

7. Analytics, advertising and other connections

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics libraries are included, but automatic analytics collection and crash-report uploads are disabled in this release. There is no analytics opt-in switch yet. We do not claim that SDK-generated records would be anonymous if reporting were enabled in a future version: such records can include identifiers and technical data.

There is no advertising SDK or personalized advertising in the current app. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Our own site pages contain no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels or remotely loaded fonts. Hosting providers still process normal web requests.

Eligible adult installs may fetch updated detection rules from Firebase. Those requests are separate from cloud history backup. Device text-to-speech and emergency calling use system services; the device speech engine may have its own online processing. External legal links open a browser; optional translated links use Google Translate, whose requests are governed by Google’s policies. You may open the original English URLs directly from the legal centre.

10. Your privacy rights and regional information

Use our privacy contact to request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your data, consent withdrawal or information about processing. We ask only for proportionate information needed to verify a request; never send a password, sign-in token or unnecessary identity document. We do not penalize you for exercising a privacy right. Some rights have legal conditions and exceptions.

  • India: applicable Indian privacy, IT and consumer requirements apply. The DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025 have phased commencement; we do not describe all provisions as already effective. As relevant provisions apply, requests can include access information, correction/completion, erasure, grievance redressal and nomination. Contact Praduman Kumar first for a grievance; this does not remove a right to approach a competent authority when available.
  • EU/EEA and UK: you may also have rights to restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to your local supervisory authority, including the UK ICO. We respond within one calendar month where required, and explain any lawful extension within that period. Consent withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing.
  • United States: state-law rights can include access/knowledge, correction, deletion, portability, an authorized-agent request, and appeal where applicable. There is no sale, cross-context advertising sharing or targeted advertising to opt out of in this release. No such processing is initiated when a Global Privacy Control signal is present. We honor applicable opt-out rights if our practices change.
  • Brazil, Canada and other countries: where applicable, request confirmation/access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal and information about sharing, and use local complaint channels. Local mandatory rights prevail over these terms.

We aim to acknowledge requests within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days, or sooner where required. A calendar-month legal deadline takes precedence over a 30-day operational target. If we need more time or retain information under an exception, we explain the reason, applicable deadline and available review route. See Data Retention & Deletion.
