FocusLock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

FocusLock helps you lock away distracting apps for a focus session you set yourself, and earn an early exit by completing a challenge. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your information. It is written to match how the app actually behaves, not to cover things it does not do.

The short version. FocusLock runs on your phone. Your sessions, challenge results and daily totals are stored on the device. If cloud backup is enabled, only counts and outcomes are copied to your private account so you can restore your history on a new phone — never the apps you block, the answers you give, your contacts, or anything the camera sees.

What stays on your device

The app's own database lives on your phone and is the source of truth. It holds your sessions, your challenge attempts, your emergency contacts, your lock profiles and your daily statistics. This information is not sent anywhere except as described under "Cloud backup" below.

Cloud backup (optional, Firebase)

So your progress survives a lost or replaced phone, the app can back up a limited copy of your history to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) under an account that is yours alone.

  • Your account is anonymous. On first launch the app signs in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This creates a random identifier. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and none is attached to your data.
  • Only you can read your data. Security rules restrict every document to the account that created it. No user can read another user's data, and neither can we use it to identify you.

Crash reports and analytics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics to understand crashes and general usage (for example, how many sessions are completed), so we can fix problems and improve the app. These reports are aggregated and are not tied to your name or identity. They never include the contents of your sessions, your challenge answers, your contacts, or camera data.

The app lock (Accessibility Service)

FocusLock uses Android's Accessibility Service for one purpose only: to know which app has just come to the front, so that an app you chose to block can be locked during a focus session.

This is an optional feature you turn on yourself, in your phone's Accessibility settings. It is the strongest way to make blocking work reliably, including on phones that stop background apps. When it is on:

  • It reads only which application is currently in the foreground — the app's package name. That is all it is technically able to receive: it is configured so it cannot retrieve window content.
  • It never reads the contents of any screen, text field, message, password, or keystroke, in FocusLock or in any other app.
  • The information it uses stays on your device. Nothing the Accessibility Service observes is ever stored beyond the moment it is used, and nothing is transmitted off the device.
  • It acts only while a focus session you started is running, and only on the apps you chose to block. You can turn it off at any time in Accessibility settings.

What this app never does

  • Read your messages, or the contents of your notifications.
  • Record or upload anything the camera sees.
  • Track your location.
  • Send your app-usage details to us or anyone else.
  • Prevent you from uninstalling it.
  • Sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Backed-up data is kept in your anonymous account so your history can be restored. To have your backed-up data deleted, contact us at focuslock@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Children

FocusLock is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
