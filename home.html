Choose a task, a focus session and the apps you want to block. Optional challenges, reel limits, website rules and progress tracking help you build a focus routine.

Focus on your terms

Use timer, Pomodoro or stopwatch sessions, saved profiles, math/reading/exercise challenges and local statistics. Android enforcement depends on the permissions you choose, your device settings and supported app/browser layouts. Blocking and counts are not guaranteed.

Understand your choices

  • Ages 13+; ages 13–17 use local tools without online accounts.
  • No ads; paid sales and automatic analytics/crash uploads are disabled in this release.
  • App cloud history backup is optional and off by default; Android system backup is separate.
  • Accessibility can read specific screen information for counting and blocking. Read the disclosure before enabling it.
  • Keep Android permission controls, uninstall and your normal emergency dialer available.
