Win back your focus.

Your phone. Your focus.

What you can do

Practical tools for a task, a study session, or a quieter break.

🔒

Block distracting apps

Choose the apps that pull you away. During a focus session, a block screen helps you keep the promise you made to yourself.

⏳

Focus your way

Choose a countdown timer, work-and-break Pomodoro cycles, or an open-ended stopwatch. Give the session a purpose.

🧠

Earn your early exit

If your session has an exit challenge, complete mental maths or a reading passage. Adults can also choose a camera-counted exercise after its separate SDK notice.

↕

Understand your scrolling

See your short-video counts and set reel limits. Supported feeds and detection depend on the app layouts on your phone.

🔥

Build a focus streak

See focused minutes, daily progress, XP and streaks. Follow the work you have done and build a routine at your own pace.

🎛

Save your setups

Keep lock profiles for Deep Work, Study, Reading or a Power Nap. Reuse the setup that fits the task in front of you.

◎

Websites & Study Mode

Add distracting websites to your blocklist and use a channel allowlist for YouTube Study Mode on supported screens.

♫

Make room for calm

Choose available focus sounds or on-device audio. Keep a session simple, with fewer reasons to pick up your phone.

✚

Keep emergency shortcuts close

Saved-contact and flashlight shortcuts remain reachable during sessions. Always keep your phone’s normal emergency dialer available.

One session at a time

How it works

01

Pick & block

Choose a purpose, a timer and the apps or websites you want to put aside.

02

Do your thing

Let the session run while you study, read, work out or get something finished.

03

Finish or earn out

See the session through, or follow the early-exit challenge you chose. Review your progress afterwards.

Understand your choices

Focus tools, with clear boundaries.

Start with local tools. Read the explanation before enabling a permission or an online feature.

Read our Privacy Policy ↗
  • No account needed for local tools. Online features are optional and restricted to adults in this release.
  • Cloud history backup is your choice. It starts off; Android’s system backup is separate.
  • You control permissions. Accessibility powers specific counting and blocking features; it remains revocable.
  • Ages 13 and over. Ages 13–17 use local tools. See the parent notice for details.

Blocking and counting depend on Android permissions, device settings and supported app layouts. REATTAIN is a personal focus aid; keep your normal emergency dialer and system controls available.
