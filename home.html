Lock the distractions.

Free · No ads · Private by design

You pick something to do and how long to do it for. For that time, the apps you chose stay shut. If you want out early, you don't just tap "quit" — you earn it by finishing a small challenge. Simple, honest, and surprisingly hard to argue your way past.

What you can do

🔒

Lock any app

Choose the apps that pull you away — Instagram, YouTube, games — and they stay locked for the whole session.

⏳

Set a focus session

Pick a purpose and a length. A clean timer counts you down; the session can't be shortened once it starts.

🧠

Earn your early exit

Leaving early takes a real effort — mental maths, a short reading passage, or a set of exercises counted by the camera.

🔥

Build a streak

Every focused day counts. A discipline score and streak turn one good session into a habit.

🎛️

Lock profiles

Save your setups — Deep Work, Study, Power Nap — and start the right one in a tap.

🆘

Emergency always on

Emergency calls and the flashlight stay reachable through the whole session, and never end it.

How it works

Pick & block

Choose what to do, how long, and which apps to lock.

Focus

The apps stay shut. Open one and a calm block screen reminds you to stay with it.

Finish or earn out

See it through — or pass a challenge if you truly need to leave early.

Private by design

  • Runs on your phone. Your sessions and stats live on the device, not on a server.
  • No ads, and nothing sold to advertisers or data brokers.
  • It never reads your screens or messages, never tracks your location, and never stops you uninstalling it.
  • The optional app lock reads only which app is in front — never anything inside it.
