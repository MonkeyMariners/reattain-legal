2. Product support

Send a short explanation, your app version and device/Android version if relevant. Include a purchase reference for billing problems, not payment-card details. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, access tokens, full device backups or unnecessary private screen captures.

3. Privacy requests and grievances

Use subject REATTAIN privacy request or REATTAIN grievance, addressed to Praduman Kumar. Tell us whether you want access, a copy, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, an objection or a review of a decision. Explain how we can identify the relevant account without disclosing unnecessary information. You can also use the dedicated account and data deletion page.

We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve or provide a substantive response within 30 days. Any shorter applicable deadline, GDPR/UK GDPR calendar-month rule or properly explained statutory extension takes priority. We explain a refusal/exception and the available review route. Requests should not require a fee, except a narrowly permitted legal exception.

4. Review and external remedies

If a response does not resolve your concern, reply with the reference and ask Praduman Kumar for review. You retain any right to complain to a competent privacy authority, consumer body or court. EU/EEA users can contact their local data-protection authority; UK users can contact the ICO. Indian statutory complaint routes depend on applicable provisions and their commencement. No mandatory arbitration or exclusive foreign forum is imposed by this contact process.

5. Security, children and community concerns

Use subjects REATTAIN security report, REATTAIN child privacy request, or REATTAIN community report as appropriate. Report a vulnerability with minimal evidence and avoid accessing another person’s data. This mailbox is not an emergency service and does not promise immediate emergency assistance.
