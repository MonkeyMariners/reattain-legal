1. What REATTAIN does

REATTAIN helps you concentrate in two ways. It counts the short videos you scroll past, so you can see the size of the habit, and it lets you lock apps and websites for a focus session you set yourself — where, if you allow it, an early exit requires completing a challenge. It also offers optional tools such as a daily reel limit, Strict Mode, Pomodoro and stopwatch timers, screen-time insights, focus sounds, streaks, statistics, lock profiles, and optional friend comparisons and shared focus rooms.

Honest limits. REATTAIN is a self-discipline tool, not a security or parental-control system, and it is not a content filter you should rely on to protect anyone else. On a normal (unrooted) Android phone you can always leave a blocked app, force-stop the App, or uninstall it.

Specifically: website blocking works only in supported browsers and cannot see inside another app's built-in browser; the adult-site filter covers a short list of well-known sites and is not exhaustive; YouTube Study Mode allows a video when no channel name is visible on screen; and reel counting is a best effort that can miss or mis-count when an app changes its layout.

If you switch on Uninstall protection, the App will send you back to your home screen if an uninstall screen opens during a session or while your daily reel limit is spent. This is a deterrent against an impulse, not a lock: you can always switch it off, disable the Accessibility Service, or uninstall in safe mode. If you switch on Strict Mode, a session cannot be ended early from inside the App — but it always ends by itself when the time you chose is up, and emergency features remain available throughout. Blocking works best when you grant the permissions the App explains, and its reliability depends on your device and its manufacturer's settings.

2. Emergency access

The App is built so that it never blocks your phone's emergency dialer, an active or incoming call, your device Settings, or the App's own emergency screen, and it offers an emergency shortcut and a flashlight during a session. Even so, you must never rely on the App in a situation where reaching help matters. If you need to make an emergency call, use your phone's normal emergency dialer directly.
