3. Features, permissions and honest limits

You choose session lengths, blocked apps/sites, challenges and permissions. Android permissions remain revocable. REATTAIN does not prevent uninstalling or disabling its service. Strict Mode can prevent an early exit from the app’s own session UI until the chosen timer ends; it does not remove Android system controls.

Blocking, reel detection, boot recovery and background operation depend on Android, device settings, manufacturer restrictions and third-party app layouts. Counts may be missed or duplicated. Website blocking works only on supported browser surfaces; a domain filter is not a complete safety filter. Study Mode may allow a video when the required channel information cannot be read. Timers, streaks and scores are estimates for personal use, not certified records or promises of performance.

4. Emergency access and physical challenges

Keep your normal emergency dialer accessible and never rely on REATTAIN to reach help. Test your device settings before a long session, particularly if calls or alerts matter. Contact numbers, torch availability, network access and emergency dialing depend on the device and your location.

Camera exercise challenges require age 18+ and acceptance of a separate notice about pose-SDK diagnostics. They are optional general fitness activities, not medical guidance or health monitoring. Choose an activity suitable for you, keep the area safe, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or distress. Camera pose detection can be inaccurate. Math and reading alternatives are available; no challenge should take priority over safety.

5. Accounts, backup and deletion

Core local tools do not require sign-in. Protect your device and Google account. Optional Firebase history backup starts only after an eligible adult enables it; Android system backup is separate. Turning backup off, signing out, deleting a social profile and uninstalling are different actions. To request account and associated server-data deletion, use the deletion page. We do not require a payment or app reinstallation to submit a request.
