FocusLock — Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

The short version: the FocusLock app uses no cookies, and these policy pages use no tracking or advertising cookies.

The app

FocusLock is a native mobile app, not a website. It does not use cookies. What it stores on your device, and the limited backup to your own private account, are described in our Privacy Policy.

These pages

The pages that host our Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and this notice are plain, static web pages. They do not set advertising or analytics cookies, do not track you across sites, and do not build a profile of you. Your browser may use its own strictly necessary storage to display the page, which is not something we control or read.

If this ever changes

If we add a feature that needs cookies — for example a website with analytics — we will update this notice first, explain what each cookie does, and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set.
