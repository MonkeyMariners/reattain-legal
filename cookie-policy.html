These pages use no app-managed cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, remotely hosted fonts or browser local storage. They are readable without JavaScript, sign-in or a cookie banner.

1. Public legal website

The REATTAIN policy site consists of static HTML, a local stylesheet and local images. We do not add non-essential cookies or tracking scripts. A banner that pretends to offer a tracking choice would not describe the current site.

2. Hosting and normal browser requests

When the site is served from GitHub Pages, GitHub processes requests needed to deliver and secure it. These can contain your IP address, browser information and request time. Caching and host-level security behavior are controlled by the hosting service and your browser. See GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not claim that a static site generates no server logs.

3. App storage is separate

The native app uses a database and preferences for sessions, permissions, age band and backup choices. These are not website cookies. Authentication credentials and provider-managed SDK storage may also exist when an adult requests online features. Turning website cookies off does not erase app data. See Data Retention & Deletion.

4. External links and translation

Following a link to Google, GitHub or another provider makes a request to that provider. A translated legal page opened through Google Translate uses Google’s service; you can use the original English pages instead. We do not control cookies that an external site sets after you leave these pages.

5. Future tracking

If non-essential website analytics, advertising or device tracking is introduced, this notice and the technical consent controls must change first. Where consent is required, those technologies must stay off until a valid choice is made, with a comparably accessible withdrawal control. Our current app has no personalized advertising, and its automatic analytics/crash uploads are disabled.
