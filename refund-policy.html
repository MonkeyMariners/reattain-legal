FocusLock — Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Refund Policy applies to paid subscriptions or purchases ("Pro") in the FocusLock app, published by Monkey Mariners. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

The core FocusLock app is free. If and when paid features are offered, the terms below apply. All purchases are made through Google Play, so Google's own refund process may also apply.

Plans

Pro is offered as a subscription — ₹49 / month or ₹399 / year, with a 7-day free trial. The current price and plan are always shown in the app before you buy. Prices may change, and promotions may apply from time to time.

Requesting a refund (Google Play / Android)

  • You may request a refund within 24 hours of a purchase or a renewal.
  • We review requests for accidental purchases, duplicate charges, and genuine technical problems that stopped you using the paid features.
  • To request a refund, email focuslock@gmail.com with the email address or account used and the approximate purchase date, or use Google Play's own "Report a problem" / refund flow.
  • Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, usually within 7 days, though the exact timing depends on Google and your bank.
  • Google Play may also handle some refunds directly, under Google's policies.

Cancelling

You can cancel a subscription at any time from your Google Play account or from the app's settings. When you cancel, your Pro features stay active until the end of the period you have already paid for; you are not charged again after that. Cancelling does not, by itself, trigger a refund for the current period — use the refund request above for that.

Outside the refund window

After the window above, and except where a refund is required by the consumer-protection law that applies to you, subscription payments are non-refundable. Nothing in this policy limits any statutory refund right you have where you live.

Contact
