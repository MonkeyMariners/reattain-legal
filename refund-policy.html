Refund & Cancellation Policy

Current free status, future checkout rules, cancellation and statutory remedies.

Version 2026-10-23 · Updated 23 October 2026

On this page

  2. 2. Cancel renewal
  3. 3. Request a refund
  4. 4. Mandatory consumer rights

Paid sales are disabled in the current REATTAIN release. No current subscription price, free trial or uniform worldwide offer is promised by this page. Local focus tools remain usable without paying.

1. If paid products are introduced

The store checkout must show the actual product, local currency price, taxes, billing period, renewal terms, any trial and cancellation instructions before purchase. For Android digital products, Google Play billing and applicable store requirements will apply. A trial applies only if it appears in the offer you accept. This policy does not activate a subscription or authorize a charge.

2. Cancel renewal

For any REATTAIN subscription you actually hold, open Google Play subscriptions, select REATTAIN and follow the cancellation steps shown for that plan. In the Play app, look under Payments & subscriptions. Cancellation normally stops a future renewal and access normally continues for the period already purchased, subject to the plan, store process and applicable rights. Check the cancellation confirmation and next billing date. Uninstalling, signing out or deleting an app account does not automatically cancel store billing.

3. Request a refund

Use Google Play’s refund route or email monkeymariners@gmail.com. Include the purchase reference/order ID, approximate date, product and explanation. Do not send card details, a password or a sign-in code. Tell us about duplicate charges, unauthorized purchases, accidental purchases or features that did not work.

Eligibility depends on the purchase, store rules and mandatory consumer law. We do not impose a universal 24-hour cutoff or guarantee approval. An approved refund follows the store/payment provider’s processing time; there is no guaranteed worldwide seven-day bank settlement. Request a store review or contact our grievance contact if you disagree with a decision.

4. Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing limits a refund, repair, replacement, price reduction, cancellation or other non-waivable remedy available under applicable law, including Indian consumer protections and applicable EU/EEA/UK digital-content/service rules. Where a cooling-off right applies, it is lost only if the legal conditions for doing so are satisfied, including any required express request/consent and acknowledgement. Using or downloading an app does not automatically waive every cancellation right.

5. Failed payments and records

Payment retries, grace periods, account holds and subscription termination depend on the actual store plan and settings. We do not promise that a failed payment can never be retried or create an amount due. Check your store account and contact support about a billing issue. The store can retain transaction records for its own legal obligations.

6. Contact

Publisher and grievance contact: Praduman Kumar / MonkeyMariners. See Contact, Support & Grievances and our purchase terms.
