Refund Policy

Last updated · 20 September 2026

This Refund Policy applies to paid subscriptions or purchases ("Pro") in the REATTAIN app, published by Monkey Mariners. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

The core REATTAIN app is free. If and when paid features are offered, the terms below apply. All purchases are made through Google Play, so Google's own refund process may also apply.

Plans

Pro is offered as a subscription — ₹25 / month or ₹229 / year (about ₹19 a month), with a 7-day free trial. The same low price applies worldwide; Google Play shows your local currency and the exact amount before you buy. Prices may change, and promotions may apply from time to time.

Requesting a refund (Google Play / Android)

  • You may request a refund within 24 hours of a purchase or a renewal.
  • We review requests for accidental purchases, duplicate charges, and genuine technical problems that stopped you using the paid features.
  • To request a refund, email reattain@gmail.com with the email address or account used and the approximate purchase date, or use Google Play's own "Report a problem" / refund flow.
  • Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, usually within 7 days, though the exact timing depends on Google and your bank.
  • Google Play may also handle some refunds directly, under Google's policies.

Cancelling — manual

You can cancel any time in Google Play → Menu → Subscriptions → REATTAIN → Cancel, or from the app's Subscription screen. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are never charged again after that. Cancelling does not, by itself, refund the current period — use the refund request above for that.

Failed payment & automatic cancellation

If a renewal payment cannot be collected — for example, insufficient balance on your payment method — Google Play retries for a short account-hold/grace period. If it still cannot be collected, the subscription is automatically cancelled and Pro is switched off. You are never charged retroactively, and no future payment is forced or queued — the subscription simply ends. You can resubscribe any time, and the free core of REATTAIN keeps working throughout.

Outside the refund window

After the window above, and except where a refund is required by the consumer-protection law that applies to you, subscription payments are non-refundable. Nothing in this policy limits any statutory refund right you have where you live.
